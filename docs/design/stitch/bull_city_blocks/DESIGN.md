## Brand & Style

The design system channels the warmth, communal charm, and rich architectural heritage of Durham, North Carolina. Built for a multiplayer social card game (supporting 2 to 10 players), the visual language creates the welcoming intimacy of a weekend tabletop game night around a sturdy wooden table.

### Design Philosophy & Aesthetic
- **Tactile Paper Craft & Modern Flat Surfaces:** Crisp, modern vector silhouettes paired with warm, matte parchment undertones. Cards avoid glossy, synthetic digital finishes in favor of an organic, lithograph-inspired presence.
- **Friendly & Approachable Structural Clarity:** Generous rounded profiles, friendly geometric forms, and high-legibility typography evoke camaraderie rather than cutthroat competitiveness.
- **Civic Pride & Neighborhood Identity:** Distinct structural frames, micro-badges, and architectural icons ground every asset in authentic Durham iconography—from historic brick warehouses and tobacco drying barns to lush forest preserves and academic Gothic masonry.
- **Game Table Legibility:** Every UI element prioritizes instant scanning across both handheld mobile viewports and communal tablet table layouts, maintaining strict 44px+ physical touch targets and high-visibility hierarchy.

## Colors

The core palette is anchored in Bull City red-clay brick and unbleached cardstock cream, complemented by an exhaustive 14-district thematic set for rapid property recognition.

### System Anchors
- **Primary (`#C84B31` - Bull City Terracotta):** Used for primary buttons, active player turns, victory emblems, and core brand actions.
- **Secondary (`#CA8A04` - Golden Tobacco Leaf):** Currency highlights, high-value set multipliers, and attention badges.
- **Tertiary (`#047857` - Forest Hills Moss):** Confirmation states, successful trade prompts, and completed district set indicators.
- **Neutral Light (`#FBF8F2` - Warm Paper Cream):** Primary tabletop surface and clean paper card face backing.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1E1B18` - Hearth Charcoal):** Evening mode felt canvas and high-contrast text rendering.

### Card Archetype Visual Signatures
- **Property Cards:** Full-width color headers corresponding to district tokens with framed property illustrations.
- **Wild Cards:** Dual or multi-segmented radial diagonal gradient headers displaying available district pairings.
- **Landmark Cards (+3 Rent):** Metallic gold borders with raised architectural corner crests.
- **Icon Cards (+4 Rent):** Deep charcoal header with iridescent foil-stamped brass badge borders.
- **Cash Cards ($1–$10):** Centered large-denomination bank engraving style with green tinted guilloche line patterns.
- **Action Cards:** Diagonal accent hazard stripes on top-right corners with high-contrast amber/brick banners.
- **Rent Cards:** 45-degree corner banners displaying applicable district color swatches.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the expressive, woodblock-style punch of **Epilogue** for card titles and score banners with the warm, open legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for action rules and descriptive text. Tactical data—such as turn timers, rent matrices, deck counters, and cash values—leverages **JetBrains Mono** for monospaced clarity.

### Rules of Usage
- **Card Titling:** Card names must be set in `headline-sm` or `headline-md` using Epilogue Bold, forced to single or tight two-line wrapping with strict optical line heights.
- **Monetary & Numeric Data:** Card values (e.g., "$3M", "+4 RENT") must be set in `label-md` or `label-lg` with monospaced numerals to preserve horizontal alignment during fast-paced play.
- **Rule Clarifications:** Descriptive rules (e.g., "Force all players to pay 2x rent on all Downtown properties") utilize `body-sm` with a minimum weight of 400.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive card table paradigm, designed to handle up to 10 players simultaneously without visual crowding.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Mobile Viewports (<640px):** Single-column layout. The active player's hand is anchored to a bottom horizontal scroll sheet (overlap offset: 24px). Opponents appear as a top scrollable ring of condensed avatar status chips. Center play arena uses a 2-column stacked grid with `gutter-mobile` (8px).
- **Tablet / Mid-Viewports (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Opponents are distributed in pairs flanking the central discard/draw decks. Player hand fans symmetrically across the bottom edge.
- **Desktop / Large Displays (>1024px):** 12-column fixed-max canvas (1440px limit). Full circular or modular table layout with 10 player stations, dedicated discard and draw piles, active trade zones, and permanent district tracking sidebars. Outer canvas padding is set to `margin-desktop` (40px).

### Spacing Guidelines
- **Card Overlap:** Cards resting in a player's hand maintain a dynamic negative overlap margin of `-1.5rem` (desktop) and `-1rem` (mobile), expanding to full width (`space-md` gap) on touch/hover focus.
- **Hit Zones:** Interactive buttons, card selection hitboxes, and action triggers must never sit closer than `space-sm` (8px) to neighboring interactive targets, strictly maintaining a minimum 44px × 44px tap zone.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines tactile card stock stacking with physical tabletop elevation, balancing flat graphic simplicity and realistic card game handling.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Game Mat / Table Surface):** Flat, textured background (#FBF8F2 or #1E1B18) with a faint 2% organic noise grain representing felt or warm craft paper.
- **Level 1 (Opponent Boards & Inactive Sets):** 1px solid border matching 12% opacity of the neutral ink color, accompanied by a resting contact shadow: `0px 2px 4px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Player Hand & Center Play Field):** Standard resting elevation for actionable cards: `0px 4px 8px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.10)`, with a crisp 1.5px border of `#E6DFD5` in light mode.
- **Level 3 (Dragged, Hovered, or Selected Cards):** Lifted physical card interaction: `0px 14px 24px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.16), 0px 2px 6px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.08)`, paired with a slight 1.04x scale transform and 2-degree tilt.
- **Level 4 (Action Resolution Modals & Trade Sheets):** Frosted ambient backdrop blur (8px) coupled with deep directional shadow: `0px 24px 48px rgba(18, 15, 12, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on softened, approachable rectangles that mimic standard rounded poker and board game cards.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Standard Cards:** 0.5rem (8px) corner radius, preserving readable corners without cutting into district color bands or numerical badges.
- **Card Containers & District Playmats (`rounded-lg`):** 1.0rem (16px) corner radius to create distinct nested framing around sets.
- **Modals, Dialogs, and Game Mats (`rounded-xl`):** 1.5rem (24px) corner radius for friendly, welcoming dialogue surfaces.
- **Counters, Currency Badges, and Player Avatars:** Fully circular/pill profile (`rounded-full`) to offer contrast against rectangular playing cards.

## Components

### 1. Game Cards
- **Card Base:** 5:7 aspect ratio card body on warm cream backing (`#FBF8F2`) with a subtle 1px inner border.
- **Header District Band:** High-contrast 28% top container fill colored by the respective neighborhood token. Holds the district name in `label-lg` white text and the district icon badge on the right.
- **Rent Matrix Table:** Clean tabular layout in `label-sm` monospaced type displaying progressive payouts (e.g., 1 card = $1, 2 cards = $3, Full Set = $7).
- **Corner Value Badges:** Circular pill badges placed at top-left and bottom-right indicating secondary cash value ($1M–$5M).

### 2. Action & Rent Cards
- **Diagonal Accent Banners:** Action cards feature a 45-degree ribbon across the top-right corner with a high-contrast label (e.g., "ACTION", "JUST SAY NO", "DEAL BREAKER").
- **Multi-District Rent Cards:** Dual-tone diagonal split headers showing two accessible district colors, permitting rent claims against either property family.

### 3. Buttons & Interaction Targets
- **Primary Action Button:** Bull City Terracotta (`#C84B31`) background with crisp white typography, 48px height, rounded-md corners, and a 2px offset drop shadow creating a physical press feel on active click.
- **Secondary / Pass Turn Button:** Warm cream background with a 2px solid terracotta border and dark charcoal text.
- **Quick Turn Timer Indicator:** Pill-shaped badge featuring a ticking circular progress SVG ring with JetBrains Mono numbers.

### 4. Player Turn & Status Chips
- **Player Tray:** Compact card containing the player's avatar, active set counts (e.g., "2/3 Sets"), total bank value, and an animated terracotta outline badge indicating the active turn.
- **District Set Completion Chip:** Mini-badges displaying the 14 neighborhood icons; filled when a player completes the entire neighborhood collection, dimmed at 20% opacity when unowned.

### 5. Selection Radios, Checkboxes & Steppers
- Custom tactile wooden-peg style toggle markers. Selected items display an inset dark terracotta dot with a high-contrast ring for accessibility.

### 6. Trading Tray & Discard Pile
- **Discard / Draw Pile:** 3D isometric stack simulation showing 3 staggered layers of cards to communicate deck depth.
- **Interactive Trade Zone:** Dashed border slot (`#CA8A04` Golden Tobacco Leaf) supporting drag-and-drop or tap-to-stage card exchanges between players.
